Why we exist

Most cloud problems are organizational before they are technical.

The pattern repeats across organizations of every size. Infrastructure is handed to one team, security to a vendor, AI to an agency, and cost to a spreadsheet somebody updates at quarter end. Each group does competent work inside its own boundary.

The failures happen between those boundaries. Controls are duplicated because nobody agrees who owns identity. Security review arrives after architecture is frozen, so findings become exceptions instead of fixes. AI pilots demo well and then stall, because no one designed the retrieval permissions, the tenant boundary or the cost model they would need in production.

TekLid is organized the other way around. The same architects who design the landing zone design the security controls, the AI retrieval model and the cost attribution — in one architecture, delivered as code, documented so your own team can carry it forward without us.

Why TekLid

Cloud, Security and AI should not be designed separately.

Most consultancies hand infrastructure, security, AI and cost management to separate teams. The result is duplicated controls, late security reviews and AI pilots that never reach production. TekLid designs all four as a single architecture, from the first landing zone to the monthly cost report.

How we work

Four commitments we hold to.

  1. 01

    Architecture before tooling

    The product choice comes after the design. We will tell you when a capability you already own is enough.

  2. 02

    Everything as code

    If it cannot be reviewed, versioned and redeployed from a repository, it is not finished.

  3. 03

    Handover is the deliverable

    Engagements end with your team able to operate what we built. Dependence on us is a design flaw, not a business model.

  4. 04

    Plain assessment

    You get the real state of the environment, including the parts that are working fine and need no spend.
